How to Find the Right Instagram Audience

A simple way to identify the communities and people most relevant to your brand.

The right audience is not simply the largest audience. It is the group of people most likely to care about your product, service, perspective, or creative work.

Start with clarity

Start with the problem you solve and the interests your best-fit audience already has. Look at relevant topics, creator communities, questions in comments, and the language people use when describing their needs.

Keep it consistent

Use these observations to guide your content and engagement. Keep outreach personal, relevant, respectful, and consistent with Instagram rules.

Build meaningful connections before chasing bigger numbers.
